# Using AI with Orbeex

AI features let you request map work in plain language. Instead of building each step by hand, you describe the goal, and Orbeex proposes the steps for you to approve.

AI is **optional**. Everything in Orbeex also works without it.

## The key idea: you approve, it executes

The AI does two things:

1. **Understands your request** — including follow-up corrections in normal language.
2. **Prepares the steps** — which data to open, what to calculate, what to produce.

The AI **never changes your data on its own**. The steps are checked by Orbeex's engine and shown to you. You approve. Only then does anything run — and it is all recorded, so it is all reversible.

## Which providers you can use

| Provider | Examples |
|---|---|
| OpenAI | GPT models |
| Anthropic | Claude models |
| Google | Gemini models |
| DeepSeek | DeepSeek models |
| Qwen | Qwen models |
| Local models | Run on your own machine — no internet at all |

You pick one, paste your own API key, and are in charge of the account and cost. Want to switch, mix, or drop AI later? That is a setting, not a commitment.

## What is and is not sent

When you make an AI-assisted request, Orbeex sends only what the AI needs to understand the task:

- **Sent:** your request in your words, plus basic facts *about* your data — column names, the coordinate system, the map area.
- **Not sent:** the map contents themselves — actual coordinates and attribute values stay on your computer unless you explicitly allow them for a specific task.

Privacy rules apply to every provider: [Your data and privacy](/your-data-and-privacy).

## Full privacy alternative: a local model

If your work is confidential — survey data, infrastructure, anything regulated — connect a local model (for example through Ollama). Then the AI runs **on your own machine** and nothing ever leaves it. You get the plain-language workflow with complete isolation.

## Guardrails that are always on

- Risky actions need explicit confirmation — with a preview of what will change.
- A spending limit per session prevents surprise AI costs.
- Every AI-proposed step is recorded in the history exactly like a manual action.

Details: [Safety and guardrails](/ai/safety).
